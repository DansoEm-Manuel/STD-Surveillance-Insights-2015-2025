# 📖 Project Overview

Sexually Transmitted Diseases (STDs) continue to pose a significant global public health challenge despite advances in prevention, diagnosis, and treatment.

This capstone project analyzes global STD surveillance data from 2015 to 2025 to understand disease prevalence, mortality trends, regional disparities, and healthcare system challenges. The goal is to transform health data into actionable insights that can support policymakers, healthcare organizations, donors, and public health stakeholders in reducing the global burden of STDs.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze global STD trends over a ten-year period.
* Identify diseases contributing most to the global burden.
* Evaluate regional differences in incidence and mortality.
* Examine Africa's disproportionate disease burden.
* Assess Ghana's STD trends and public health outlook.
* Identify healthcare system gaps affecting outcomes.
* Provide evidence-based recommendations for reducing disease burden.

# 📊 Analysis Breakdown

## Global Disease Burden Analysis

This section evaluates the overall magnitude of STD cases and deaths worldwide.

### Key Findings

| Metric                  | Value         |
| ----------------------- | ------------- |
| Global STD Cases        | 336.9 Million |
| Lives Lost              | 15.7 Million  |
| Most Affected Age Group | 25–49 Years   |

### Insight

The global burden of STDs remains extremely high, with economically active populations experiencing the greatest impact.

---

## Global Trend Analysis

This analysis examines how STD cases have changed over the last decade.

### Key Findings

* Annual cases fluctuate between 25M and 39M.
* No sustained downward trend was observed.
* Reported cases remain persistently high.

### Insight

Current interventions are managing STD transmission rather than significantly reducing it.

---

## Disease Profile Analysis

This section identifies diseases responsible for the largest share of the global burden.

### Major Diseases

* HIV/AIDS
* Hepatitis B
* Human Papillomavirus (HPV)
* Syphilis

### Insight

A small group of diseases contributes to the majority of global STD cases and deaths.

---

## Africa Burden Analysis

Africa experiences the highest concentration of severe STD outcomes globally.

### Key Findings

| Metric                 | Value      |
| ---------------------- | ---------- |
| Cases                  | 80 Million |
| Deaths                 | 4 Million  |
| Highest Burden Country | Nigeria    |

### Insight

Healthcare access challenges, late-stage diagnosis, and socioeconomic barriers contribute significantly to mortality rates.

---

## Country-Level Analysis

The analysis identified countries with the highest disease burden.

### High-Burden Countries

* Nigeria
* South Africa
* Kenya
* Ghana

### Insight

Targeting interventions in high-burden countries could significantly reduce overall continental disease impact.

---

## Ghana Deep Dive

A focused analysis of STD trends and healthcare challenges in Ghana.

### Key Findings

* STD rates fluctuated throughout the decade.
* HIV/AIDS remains a major contributor to disease burden.
* Hepatitis B trends show signs of stabilization.
* Prevention efforts have produced mixed results.

### Insight

Ghana demonstrates both encouraging progress and persistent public health vulnerabilities.

---

## Future Outlook Analysis

This section explores possible outcomes based on intervention effectiveness.

### If Current Trends Continue

* Cases remain persistently high.
* Mortality remains elevated.
* Healthcare system strain increases.

### If Interventions Improve

* Mortality declines.
* Prevention programs become more effective.
* Healthcare outcomes improve significantly.

### Insight

The future burden of STDs is highly responsive to policy decisions, funding, and healthcare investments.

---

# 🔍 Key Insights

* Over 336 million STD cases were recorded during the study period.
* HIV/AIDS remains the leading contributor to prevalence and mortality.
* Africa bears a disproportionate share of severe outcomes.
* Healthcare system gaps strongly influence mortality rates.
* Early detection remains one of the most effective intervention strategies.
* High-burden countries present opportunities for targeted impact.
* Data-driven interventions can significantly improve future outcomes.

---

# 💡 Recommendations

### Strengthen Early Detection

* Expand routine STD screening programs.
* Improve surveillance systems.

### Increase Vaccination Coverage

* Scale HPV vaccination programs.
* Improve Hepatitis B immunization rates.

### Target High-Burden Regions

* Prioritize interventions in Nigeria and similar high-burden settings.
* Allocate resources based on disease burden.

### Improve Healthcare Access

* Increase treatment availability.
* Reduce barriers to early diagnosis and care.

### Support Long-Term Funding

* Invest in sustainable prevention and treatment programs.
* Strengthen healthcare infrastructure.
